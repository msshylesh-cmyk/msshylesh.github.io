Dr. M. S. Shylesh Chandran is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at the Amrita School of Physical Sciences, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (Amritapuri Campus, Kerala). He earned his Ph.D. and M.Sc. from the School of Environmental Sciences, Mahatma Gandhi University, Kerala.

Prior to joining Amrita, he served as Project Scientist B in the Marine Geoscience Group at the MoES–National Centre for Earth Science Studies (NCESS) and as Scientist at the KSCSTE–Institute for Climate Change Studies (ICCS).

His interdisciplinary research focuses on wetland geochemistry, priority pollutants, microplastics, blue carbon sequestration, and climate change mitigation, bridging field-based environmental monitoring with geochemical risk assessment and coastal conservation.

Focus & Inquiry

Research Thrust Areas

Investigating biogeochemical dynamics, contaminant pathways, and climate resilience across tropical wetlands, estuaries, and coastal ecosystems.

Environmental Geochemistry & Pollution Management

Trace metal speciation, mercury dynamics, sediment core enrichment histories, and remediation strategies in estuarine and wetland matrices.

Climate Change & Blue Carbon Sequestration

Quantifying ecosystem carbon stocks in mangrove forests, organic carbon burial records, urban warming trends, and nature-based climate mitigation.

Ecotoxicology & Environmental Risk Assessment

Bioaccumulation of pesticides and microplastics, multi-biomarker ecotoxicological responses, and human & ecological health risk modeling.

Coastal Processes & Coastal Zone Management

Estuarine sediment dynamics, near-shore marine geoscience, Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) assessments, and sustainable shoreline stewardship.

Ecology & Biodiversity Conservation

Biodiversity estimation in Western Ghats biosphere reserves, wetland habitat ecology, soil fauna diversity, and agroecosystem conservation.

Remote Sensing & Geoinformatics (GIS)

Multi-decadal land-use/land-cover change analysis, spatial pollutant mapping, and geospatial modeling of coastal and urban landscapes.
